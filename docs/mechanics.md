# Game Mechanics & Systems Architecture

This document outlines how the core simulation systems—**Buildings**, **Economy**, **Policies**, **Monthly Events**, and **Faction Approvals**—interact with one another in *Be the Mayor*.

---

## 1. Core Simulation Loop & Time
- **Monthly Ticks**: The game runs on a 12-month campaign cycle (January to December). At normal speed (`1x`), each in-game month takes 12 seconds. Players can also **Pause** time at any point to plan their city.
- **Monthly Execution (`EconomyManager` & `EventManager`)**: At the end of every month:
  1. All city structures are evaluated.
  2. Financial income and upkeep expenses are computed and applied to the treasury.
  3. Faction approval shifts are calculated based on city conditions and enacted policies.
  4. An EU4-style strategic event or dilemma popup is triggered, requiring a mayoral decision before time resumes.

---

## 2. Buildings & Zoning (`BUILDINGS` in `Constants.ts`)
Buildings form the physical foundation of the city grid. Each building type defines specific production and consumption parameters:

| Building Type | Category | Cost | Upkeep | Population | Jobs | Utility Supply | Utility Demand | Pollution | Tax Bonus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Residential House** | Residential | $100 | $5 | 4 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 1 | $30 |
| **Industrial Factory** | Industrial | $250 | $15 | 0 | 6 | 0 | 5 | 8 | $80 |
| **Coal Power Plant** | Utility | $200 | $20 | 0 | 2 | 40 | 0 | 10 | $0 |
| **Green Energy Station**| Utility | $450 | $25 | 0 | 2 | 40 | 0 | 0 | $0 |
| **Public Park** | Civic | $150 | $10 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 | -8 | $0 |

- **Utility Balance**: Total utility supply from power plants must equal or exceed total utility demand from houses, factories, and parks. Unpowered buildings display an active warning badge (`⚡ NO PWR`).
- **Pollution**: Accumulates from dirty factories and coal power plants, mitigated by parks and green energy policies. High pollution lowers resident happiness and triggers environmentalist backlash.

---

## 3. Economy & Finances (`EconomyManager.ts`)
Every month, the economy calculates net income using the formula:
$$\text{Net Income} = \text{Tax Revenue} - \text{Building Upkeep} - \text{Policy Upkeep}$$

- **Tax Revenue**: Generated by residential houses and industrial factories, scaled dynamically by the Income Tax Rate policy multiplier ($\text{Tax Rate} / 10$).
- **Building Upkeep**: Sum of ongoing maintenance costs for all placed structures.
- **Policy Upkeep**: Sum of active national/municipal mandates and subsidies.

---

## 4. Policy Matrix & Democracy (`PolicyManager.ts`)
Players can adjust legislation through the **Policy Council Dashboard**:

1. **Income Tax Rate** (Slider from 0% to 25%):
   - Higher taxes increase revenue but decrease **Residents** and **Business Tycoons** approval.
   - Lower taxes boost their approval at the cost of treasury income.
2. **Green Energy Mandate** ($50/mo upkeep):
   - Boosts **Environmentalist** approval while slightly taxing **Tycoons**, and directly reduces city pollution.
3. **Industrial Subsidies** ($60/mo upkeep):
   - Boosts **Business Tycoons** and job output (+15%), but increases pollution and draws disapproval from **Environmentalists**.
4. **Public Transit Funding** ($40/mo upkeep):
   - Boosts **Labor Union** and **Residents** approval, reduces pollution, and grants a minor bonus to overall citizen happiness.

---

## 5. Faction Approval & Mayor Rating (`GameState.ts`)
The city's political stability rests on four key interest groups:
- **Environmentalists** (`env`)
- **Business Tycoons** (`tycoon`)
- **Labor Union** (`labor`)
- **General Residents** (`residents`)

- **Overall Mayor Approval**: Calculated as the mathematical average of all four faction approval ratings ($0 - 100\%$):
  $$\text{Mayor Approval} = \frac{\text{Env} + \text{Tycoon} + \text{Labor} + \text{Residents}}{4}$$
- **Game Over / Report Card**: At the end of Month 12, the final Mayor Approval score determines the final Letter Grade (A+ to F) on the Year-End Report Card.

---

## 6. Monthly Events & Dilemmas (`EventManager.ts`)
At the end of each month, a random event is drawn from the `EVENT_DATABASE` (e.g., *Industrial Chemical Spill*, *Labor Strike*, *Green Innovation Opportunity*). 
- Each event presents 2 distinct mayoral choices.
- Choosing an option immediately impacts **Treasury Money**, specific **Faction Approvals**, **Pollution**, or **Citizen Happiness**, creating deep strategic trade-offs between economic growth, environmental health, and political survival.
